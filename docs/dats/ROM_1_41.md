# ROM/1/41.DAT — Lower Jeuno (zone)

**Path:** `ROM/1/41.DAT`
**Type:** Zone DAT (mesh + placement + textures + **effects** + sound)
**Size:** 8,804,400 bytes (0x866C70), ~1934 sections
**Area:** **Lower Jeuno** (in-game `=== Area: Lower Jeuno ===`)

This is our primary working zone for [[zone-export]] / [[zone-import]] (mesh-merge,
placement edits) and now effects. See `docs/zone/format.md` for the full section
format and `docs/fx/effects.md` for the effect (`0x05`) structure.

## Effects / generators (`0x05`) — full catalog

`0x05` sections are **unencrypted** (meta mode byte = 0) FourCC-named generator
resources: particle effects, lights, point lights, sky effects, etc. They carry
their own local position and reference meshes/textures/sub-effects by name. Format
documented in `docs/fx/effects.md`.

### The fountain effect stack (3 layers)

The central fountain has **three `0x05` effect layers**, all clustered around the
`funsui` basin (placement at raw `-15.73, 0, 5.23`). Each effect names a mesh and
gives it a local position:

| Effect(s) | places mesh | what it is | Y level |
|-----------|-------------|------------|---------|
| `tki1`–`tki5` | `sibj` (sibjun3) | splash water **jets** (5, one per spout) | -7.20 |
| `awa1`–`awa6` | `awan` | **bubbles** (6) | -0.42 |
| `grid` | `suim` (suimen, 水面 "water surface") | the **puddle** / basin water | -0.39 |

Per-jet positions (the float-triple after the `sibj` reference, at body +0xD4):

| Effect | offset | local position (x, y, z) |
|--------|--------|--------------------------|
| `tki5` | 0x110970 | -17.86, -7.20, 5.41 |
| `tki4` | 0x110b40 | -16.80, -7.20, 7.06 |
| `tki3` | 0x1115b0 | -16.29, -7.20, 3.96 |
| `tki2` | 0x111780 | -14.69, -7.20, 4.15 |
| `tki1` | 0x111d10 | -14.51, -7.20, 5.26 |

Each `tki` is 0x1D0 bytes and references the splash mesh **`sibj`** and sub-effect
**`tkus`** (a `0x19`). Spray textures are `0x20` sections **`funsui_abuk`** (foam,
*abuku*), **`funsui_sib1`** (splash, *shibuki*), **`funsui_umi02`** (water),
**`funsui_tare`** (drip, *tare*).

**Orphan meshes (why they're invisible in zone export):** `sibj`, `awan`, `suim`
are `0x2E` meshes that have **no `0x1C` placement** — they are positioned solely by
their effect generator. `xi zone export` only assembles `0x1C`-placed objects, so
these never appear in the exported GLB. (ROM/1/41 has 64 such orphan meshes — also
sky `sunsphere`/`moon`/`star`/`cld`/`kamome`, and `lowsea`/`2lowsea` ocean.)

Remove the whole fountain effect set with: `xi fx delete ROM/1/41 tki awa grid`
(verified: DAT stays valid, 1934 → 1922 sections, loads in-game with the fountains
present but no water).
